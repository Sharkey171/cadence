Cadence privacy policy

Last updated: 11 November 2026

Cadence is a music player for your own music files, with optional access to free podcasts, internet radio, free music and YouTube. This page explains what happens to your information when you use it.

The short version

  • Cadence has no accounts, no adverts of its own, no analytics and no tracking.
  • The people who make Cadence do not receive, collect or store any information about you or what you listen to.
  • Your music, playlists, likes, listening history and settings are stored only on your own device.

What stays on your device

The music files you add, the covers and lyrics saved for them, your playlists, liked songs, listening history, downloaded episodes and settings are kept in the app's own storage on your device. They are never uploaded. Removing the app or clearing its data deletes them.

When Cadence contacts other services

Cadence only contacts another service when you use a feature that needs it. Each request goes directly from your device to that service, which will see your device's internet address, as any website does. Cadence sends only what the feature needs:

  • Lyrics (LRCLIB): the song's title and artist.
  • Covers and podcasts (Apple's public music and podcast directory): the song's artist, album and title, or your podcast search words.
  • Artist biographies (Wikipedia): the artist's name.
  • Radio (Radio Browser): your station search words and chosen country or genre. Playing a station connects you to that station's own stream.
  • Free music (Internet Archive): your search words, and the recordings you play or download.
  • Podcasts: playing or downloading an episode, or adding a show by its feed address, connects you to that podcast's own host.
  • YouTube (Google): the videos you play are shown by YouTube's own embedded player, which is covered by Google's privacy policy and the YouTube Terms of Service. By default the player runs in YouTube's privacy-enhanced mode, which does not use your YouTube sign-in. If you switch on "Use my YouTube sign-in" in Settings, the player uses the YouTube account signed in to your browser, and YouTube can associate what you play with that account. If you add your own YouTube API key, your search words are sent to Google. If you connect your Google account, Cadence reads the list of your YouTube playlists and liked videos to show them to you; it cannot change anything in your account, the sign-in lasts about an hour, and nothing from your account is stored outside your device.

Looking up covers, lyrics and biographies can be switched off in Settings under "Online lookups". The other features contact a service only when you open or use them.

Keys you enter

If you enter a YouTube API key or a Google client ID, it is stored only on your device and is sent only to Google.

Children

Cadence is not directed at children and collects no information from anyone.
